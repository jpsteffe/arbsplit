Race Arbitrage Calculator

Different betting sites sometimes disagree on a horse's odds. When they do, you can split your money across several horses so you make a profit no matter which one wins. Enter the odds below and this tool does the math.

New to horse racing? Quick glossary
Win
A bet that pays only if your horse finishes 1st. This is the simplest bet and the only one you need.
Place
Pays if your horse finishes 1st or 2nd.
Show
Pays if your horse finishes in the top 3.
Odds
How much a bet pays. At +400 (also written 4/1 or 5.00), a $10 bet returns $50: your $10 back plus $40 profit.
Program number
The number printed in the race program for each horse, such as #1 or #7.
